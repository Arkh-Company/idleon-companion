# Formula trees come from the validated catalog

Only the 289 numbers with destination `arvore` in `docs/catalogo/numeros.json` have a formula tree, down from 716. The other numbers of the catalog stay recorded there with their destination (inside another tree, farm panel, planner, current target and the like) for future features. A number that opened a tree in 0.9 may no longer open one: the catalog was validated number by number against rules that keep only standardized numbers formed by several sources (spec 010, FR-001; `docs/catalogo/regras.md`).
